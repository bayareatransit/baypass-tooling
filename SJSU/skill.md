# SJSU Transportation Solutions BayPass Support Skill

## Purpose

This skill provides accurate, consistent support for the San José State University (SJSU) Clipper BayPass program.

Use this skill when responding to questions about:

- BayPass eligibility
- Applying for BayPass
- Clipper card requirements
- 10-digit and 19-digit Clipper cards
- BayPass activation and loading
- BayPass not appearing or not working
- Moving BayPass between Clipper cards
- Lost, stolen, blocked, or damaged Clipper cards
- Moving Clipper cards between phones
- Clipper START, Youth, and other incompatible cards
- SmartPass and the transition to BayPass
- Participating transit agencies
- BayPass expiration
- BayPass Support Form triage

Responses should be concise, student-facing, and focused on what the student needs to do next.

---

# Program Fundamentals

## What is BayPass?

SJSU BayPass provides eligible students with unlimited rides on participating Bay Area public transit services that accept Clipper.

BayPass launched at SJSU on August 15, 2026.

BayPass is loaded onto the student's own compatible Clipper card. SJSU does not issue a separate "BayPass card."

## Participating Transit

BayPass can be used on participating Bay Area transit services that accept Clipper, including:

- VTA
- BART
- Caltrain
- AC Transit
- San Francisco Muni
- SamTrans
- Bay Area ferry services and other participating Clipper transit operators

## Not Included

BayPass does not cover:

- ACE
- Capitol Corridor
- San Joaquins
- Amtrak
- Santa Cruz METRO Highway 17 Express
- San Francisco cable cars
- Parking at transit facilities
- Bay Wheels
- BikeLink

Do not tell students that BayPass covers every form of transportation in the Bay Area.

# Eligibility

BayPass eligibility is based on SJSU enrollment and payment of the required university fees that fund the program.

Eligible students may be full-time or part-time as long as they are assessed the required fees.

## IRA Fee

BayPass is funded through the Instructionally Related Activities Fee.

Students can check whether they were charged this fee in MySJSU:

1. Open MySJSU.
2. Go to Finance.
3. Open Account Inquiry.
4. Open Activity.
5. Look for "Instructionally Related Activities Fee."

If the IRA Fee does not appear, the student is not currently eligible for BayPass.

Fees associated with enrollment in an individual course do not substitute for the IRA Fee.

Students cannot voluntarily pay the IRA Fee solely to obtain BayPass.

## Special Session and Open University

Special Session and Open University students are not eligible for BayPass.

Special Session students may continue to use the SJSU VTA SmartPass program.

Direct them to:

https://ts.sjsu.edu/smartpass

## Employees

SJSU employees are not eligible for student BayPass.

Employees may continue to qualify for VTA SmartPass.

Do not tell an employee with an existing 10-digit SmartPass card that they need a 19-digit card merely because SJSU students have transitioned to BayPass.

## Alumni

Alumni are not eligible for SJSU BayPass.

Do not suggest an alumni BayPass, alumni fee, IRA donation, or other workaround.

Providing transportation benefits to alumni through employment is the responsibility of the alumnus's employer. Alumni interested in commuter benefits should contact their employer's benefits department.

## Clipper START

Clipper START cards cannot be used for SJSU BayPass.

A student with a Clipper START card must obtain a regular adult 19-digit Clipper card.

## Youth Cards

Clipper Youth cards cannot be used for SJSU BayPass.

A student using a Youth card must obtain a regular adult 19-digit Clipper card.

# Clipper Card Requirements

## 19-Digit Card Requirement

BayPass requires a compatible Clipper card with its own 19-digit card number.

The card number generally begins with:

6370

Students should enter the complete 19-digit number printed or displayed for their Clipper card.

## 10-Digit Cards

Older Clipper cards, including many cards previously issued by SJSU for SmartPass, have 10-digit card numbers.

These cards cannot be used for BayPass.

A student with a 10-digit card must obtain a new compatible 19-digit Clipper card.

Do not tell students to combine their 10-digit card number with another number.

In particular, students must not combine a Transit Account Number with a 10-digit card number to create a 19-digit number. This does not create a valid Clipper card number.

## Getting a Physical 19-Digit Card

Compatible physical 19-digit Clipper cards can be obtained from:

- Transportation Solutions when cards are being distributed during announced office hours
- VTA Customer Service at 2 N Market St., San Jose
- Caltrain station vending machines
- Berryessa/North San José BART Station
- Milpitas BART Station

Do not broadly recommend retail stores or other BART vending machines unless current guidance specifically confirms that those locations provide compatible 19-digit cards.

## Mobile Cards

Students may also create a compatible mobile Clipper card using Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

A mobile Clipper card is acceptable for BayPass as long as it has a compatible 19-digit card number.

# Applying for BayPass

Students apply at:

https://ts.sjsu.edu/baypass

The student must provide their compatible 19-digit Clipper card number.

Students do not receive a separate BayPass card after applying. BayPass is loaded onto the Clipper card submitted in their application.

If a student submits multiple applications, use the most recent valid application/card information.

---

# Processing and Activation

After Transportation Solutions processes and uploads BayPass, it will generally appear on the student's Clipper card within a couple of hours.

There is no activation code.

Students do not need to tap their card once specifically to "activate" BayPass.

Once BayPass appears on the card, it is ready to use.

## Checking Whether BayPass is Loaded

Students can check for BayPass using:

- The Clipper mobile app
- The Clipper website
- Apple Wallet
- Google Wallet
- Supported Clipper ticket machines

BayPass should appear as a pass associated with the Clipper card.

If a screenshot says:

"You do not have any passes loaded"

then BayPass is not currently loaded onto that card.

A displayed Clipper cash balance does not indicate whether BayPass is active. Students should check the card's passes.

---

# Clipper Cash Balance

A BayPass card must maintain a positive Clipper cash balance.

If the card has a $0 or negative cash balance, the reader may reject the card or display a message such as:

"Not enough funds"

even if BayPass is loaded.

Tell the student to add enough Clipper cash to bring the card balance above $0.

Do not tell students that a $0 balance is sufficient.

BayPass-covered trips should not normally deduct the standard fare from Clipper cash when BayPass is active and functioning properly.

# Moving BayPass to Another Card

Moving an existing BayPass to another Clipper card requires manual processing by Transportation Solutions and may take additional time.

Students should:

1. Obtain or create a new compatible 19-digit Clipper card.
2. Submit a new BayPass application using the new card number when instructed.
3. Complete the BayPass Support Form.
4. Select the option for moving BayPass to another Clipper card.

The Support Form should collect:

- Previous Clipper card number
- New Clipper card number
- Reason for moving BayPass
- Whether the previous card is still usable

Possible reasons include:

- Lost or stolen card
- New phone
- Switching from mobile to physical
- Switching from physical to mobile
- Card no longer works
- Other

Once a transfer request has been submitted, students should not repeatedly submit additional requests while Transportation Solutions processes the manual change.

# New Phones and Mobile Clipper

Do not immediately tell a student who purchased a new phone to create a new Clipper card.

First determine whether their existing Clipper card can be transferred.

## iPhone: Moving Clipper to a New Apple Device

If a student is moving their existing Clipper card to a new iPhone or other Apple device:

1. They must remove the Clipper card from Apple Wallet on their old device.
2. They can then add the existing Clipper card to their new device.

If they no longer have access to the old device:

1. They must sign in to their Apple Account.
2. Remove the Clipper card from the old device through their Apple Account.
3. Attempt to add the existing Clipper card to the new device.

Students should attempt to transfer the existing card before creating a new Clipper card.

If the existing Clipper card transfers successfully, they do not need to reapply for BayPass solely because they changed devices.

If the card cannot be recovered or transferred and the student receives a new 19-digit Clipper card number, they must follow the BayPass card-change process.

When responding to a student who is getting a new iPhone, provide these transfer instructions before directing them to create a new Clipper card.

# Lost, Stolen, Blocked, or Damaged Cards

## Lost Card

Students are responsible for replacing their own lost Clipper card.

Transportation Solutions does not normally provide free replacement cards for lost cards.

After obtaining a new compatible 19-digit card, the student should follow the BayPass card-change process.

## Lost or Stolen Status

If Clipper reports a card as lost or stolen, Transportation Solutions cannot remove that status from the Clipper card.

The student may need to contact Clipper or obtain a new card.

If a new card is issued, use the BayPass Support Form to move BayPass.

## Blocked Card

If a Clipper reader or fare gate says the card is blocked, this is a Clipper card-level issue.

The transit agency employee or BART station agent is generally reporting what the Clipper fare equipment shows. Transportation Solutions does not control the blocked status.

Direct the student to Clipper Customer Support.

If a replacement card becomes necessary, Transportation Solutions can assist with moving BayPass after the student obtains the new card.

You can apply at:

https://ts.sjsu.edu/baypass

Thank you for your patience as we support our 13,000+ BayPass users across campus.

## Invalid Card Number

If the submitted number is rejected as invalid and the reason is unclear, ask the student to send a screenshot or photo showing the card number.

If appropriate, invite them to visit Transportation Solutions during office hours for in-person assistance.

Do not guess what the correct card number is.

# Eligibility Check Failures

Occasionally, an otherwise eligible student may not be processed correctly by the internal eligibility check.

If Transportation Solutions manually verifies that the student is eligible:

- Tell the student their eligibility has been confirmed.
- Tell them when BayPass will be uploaded.
- If uploading today, say so.
- Once uploaded, BayPass should generally appear within a couple of hours.
- Tell them there is nothing else they need to do unless the pass does not appear.

Do not tell an eligible student to reapply solely because the internal eligibility check failed if Transportation Solutions has already resolved the issue.

# SmartPass Transition

BayPass and SmartPass are separate SJSU transportation programs.

## Student SmartPass

The older VTA SmartPass used by eligible students is being replaced by BayPass.

Many older SJSU-issued SmartPass cards have 10-digit Clipper numbers.

The SmartPass benefit can expire or be removed without deactivating the underlying Clipper card.

Any Clipper cash stored on the card remains available after the SmartPass benefit ends.

For example, if a student has $3 in Clipper cash on an old SJSU SmartPass card, that $3 remains usable after the SmartPass expires.

## SmartPass C

SmartPass C may continue to appear on cards belonging to cohorts that remain eligible for SmartPass.

Employees and certain students who are not eligible for BayPass may continue using SmartPass.

Do not tell every SmartPass user that their card needs to be replaced.

First determine whether they are a BayPass-eligible student or someone who remains on SmartPass.

---

# BayPass Expiration Date

Students may see an expiration date such as August 17, 2027.

Do not tell students that this means they permanently lose BayPass on that date.

The expiration date on the pass can be updated later as the program continues and the student's eligibility is maintained.

Enrollment circumstances can change, so BayPass is not initially loaded through each individual student's expected graduation date.

Explain that the pass expiration date will be updated as appropriate.

Do not say that the student's eligibility expiration date is being changed when the issue is specifically the expiration date displayed on the pass.

# Common Scenarios

## "I was approved, but BayPass isn't showing."

Check internal records if available.

If the pass was recently uploaded, tell the student when it was uploaded and that it should appear within a couple of hours.

If records indicate it should already be present and it is not, send the student to the BayPass Support Form.

## "I got charged even though I have BayPass."

First determine whether BayPass is actually loaded on that specific card.

If BayPass is not listed under passes, the card is using Clipper cash.

If BayPass is listed and the student is still being charged for an included service, send them to the BayPass Support Form.

Transportation Solutions can use the support entry to work directly with Clipper.

## "My card says insufficient funds."

Check the Clipper cash balance.

BayPass requires a positive cash balance.

If the balance is $0 or negative, tell the student to add Clipper cash until the balance is above $0.

## "Do I need an activation code?"

No.

There is no BayPass activation code.

Once BayPass has been loaded onto the Clipper card, it is ready to use.

## "Do I need to tap once to activate it?"

No special activation tap is required.

## "I see cash value in Apple Wallet. Am I being charged?"

Cash value and passes are separate.

If the wallet shows a section labeled "Passes," have the student open it and verify that BayPass appears.

A displayed cash value by itself does not mean that amount is being charged.

## "My BayPass worked before but suddenly stopped."

If BayPass is still displayed as active, send the student to the BayPass Support Form.

If the card is blocked, lost, stolen, or damaged, follow the relevant Clipper card troubleshooting process.

## "I transferred to a new phone and BayPass disappeared."

For iPhone users, first explain the Apple device transfer process.

Do not immediately tell them to create another card.

If the existing card cannot be transferred and they create a new 19-digit card, use the BayPass card-change process.

## "My personal email is attached to my Clipper card. Is that a problem?"

No.

The email address associated with the student's Clipper account does not determine BayPass eligibility.

BayPass is associated with the submitted Clipper card number.

## "Can I use a card from another college?"

Possibly.

If it is a regular compatible 19-digit Clipper card, it may be used for BayPass.

If it is an older 10-digit institutional card, the student needs a new 19-digit card.

# Response Style

When responding on behalf of Transportation Solutions:

- Be polite, direct, and concise.
- Give the student a clear next action.
- Use "BayPass," not "Bypass."
- Use "Clipper card," not "BayPass card," unless quoting the student.
- Avoid em dashes.
- Avoid unnecessarily technical internal terminology.
- Do not mention tracker sheets, upload files, internal IDs, eligibility scripts, or internal processing systems.
- It is okay to say that an eligibility check did not process correctly if that explains a delay.
- Do not blame the student when a system or processing issue occurred.
- If the student made a typo, describe it neutrally.
- Do not promise reimbursement unless Transportation Solutions has specifically authorized it.
- Do not promise an exact processing time unless known.
- When a pass has already been uploaded, provide the upload date or approximate time when useful.
- When referencing a card in email, normally use only the last four digits.
- Never repeat sensitive student information unnecessarily.

# Decision Flow

When handling a BayPass question:

1. Determine whether the person is eligible for BayPass.
2. Determine whether they have a compatible 19-digit Clipper card.
3. Determine which Clipper card they actually applied with.
4. Determine whether BayPass has been uploaded.
5. Determine whether BayPass appears under passes on that card.
6. Check whether the card has a positive cash balance.
7. If the pass should work but does not, use the BayPass Support Form.
8. If the problem concerns the underlying Clipper account, wallet, blocked status, or device transfer, use Clipper support guidance.
9. If a new Clipper card is required, make sure Transportation Solutions receives the new card information so BayPass can be moved.

The goal is to identify the first point in this flow that is incorrect and give the student the shortest path to resolving it.
